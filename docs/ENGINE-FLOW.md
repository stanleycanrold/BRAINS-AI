## The pipeline: Idea → Evidence → Wedge → First customers

### Stage 0 — Capture the idea
- Founder inputs: the problem, who it's for, what they believe, and why now.
- Engine turns it into a **structured hypothesis** and surfaces the **riskiest assumptions**.
- Output: a hypothesis card + a ranked assumption list.

### Stage 1 — Validate with evidence
- Guided **customer interviews** (AI-generated, non-leading prompts based on the hypothesis).
- **Demand experiments**: waitlist, landing test, concierge/pilot offer.
- **Signal scoring**: every response scored weak / medium / strong by commitment.
- Output: a scored evidence log + a kill / pivot / build recommendation.

### Stage 2 — Offer & positioning
- Turn validated insight into a **clear promise** (who / change / why now).
- Test whether buyers can repeat it back.
- Output: an offer line + positioning that buyers understand in one breath.

### Stage 3 — Shape the 0→1 launch path
- Scope the **one-job MVP** from proven demand (not the dream roadmap).
- Define the **launch surface** + the **first-customer motion**.
- Output: a thin build plan + a launch/outreach motion.

### Stage 4 — First customers & traction signals
- Run the customer motion (list → message → next step → follow-up → log → update).
- Track the metrics that matter at 0→1: conversations, pilots, activation, early revenue.
- Output: a live traction dashboard + push / pivot / stop guidance.
